# HSHH-robot facts
> Verified: 2026-08-14
> Sources: <https://github.com/hzhang29-droid/HsHH-robot>, <https://www.hshhrobotie.space>, `docs/HSHH-robot_PRD.md`, repository `README.md`

## Existence and status
- Status: in-development hackathon prototype with a publicly accessible Robotie front-end on Vercel; not presented as a released consumer hardware product.
- PRD version: v0.4, marked `P0 Build Ready` on 2026-08-14.
- Primary language and scope: single-user, single-device, Chinese-first P0 demonstration.

## Product facts used by the interface
- Core experience: notice the user, ask for consent, approach safely, invite a hug, react to being held, and remember a confirmed preference.
- Avatar flow: upload one JPEG, PNG, or WebP pet photo up to 8 MB; generate one identity layer and a 45-frame pack covering 9 expressions.
- Expression set: idle, noticed, listening, thinking, happy, confused, sad, sleeping, and angry.
- Safety: movement requires explicit consent and fresh local sensor checks; stop and release actions cannot be refused.
- Privacy: original pet photos are temporary and must not enter Git or long-term logs; memories are visible, correctable, deletable, and pausable.
